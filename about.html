About Leka

Based out of Albuquerque, New Mexico, Leka brings a sharp, nerdy observer's eye to stand-up comedy.

Blending wit with real-world technical perspective, the routines dive into the hilarious chaos of everyday life, modern quirks, and finding the punchlines in unexpected places.
