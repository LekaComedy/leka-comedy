About Me

Stand Up Comedian. Musical performer. Anxious.

Based in Albuquerque, NM, Leka is a Latina/Chicana stand-up comedian whose tightly woven joke writing connects the dots of her unconventional life. Growing up as one of seven with out-of-touch Hispanic parents, including a spicy Latina mom, she now balances a full-time job with raising three neurodivergent kids. Though she occasionally dabbles in sketch and improv, stand-up is where she solves the mysteries of her own brain. Blending clever storytelling, sharply timed callbacks, and musical comedy, she tackles everything from dating red flags and the healthcare system to getting older and full-time #momlyfe.

The Origin Story

Starting out as a band nerd in high school, Leka spent years immersed in the local music scene. Exploring everything from rock to hip-hop shaped her unique voice as a musical comedian, allowing her to bring a musician's sense of timing to every punchline.

Whether she is on stage at a comedy club or a festival, Leka brings her whole self to every performance. Laughter might just be what the doctor ordered, official diagnosis not included.
